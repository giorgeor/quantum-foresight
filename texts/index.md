## Conflicting dates and records

- Q-Con (11 September) came before the formal NI report launch (23 September): the findings were presented first, the report published later.
- The Q-Con programme spells the firm 'Quantum Forsight'; the correct spelling is Quantum Foresight.
- SAFIRE has no publication date in SAMI's own post. Search snippets suggest 2019 for the project and autumn 2021 for the report; neither is confirmed on a page, so the work is left undated.
- SAMI's SAFIRE post says the scenarios were reviewed within the Directorate-General and third party endorsed; it does not say 'peer-reviewed'.
- The Futures Toolkit has a 2017 edition and a 2023 refresh; SAMI's role is documented for the 2023 refresh.
- The misuse assessment has no date in the public record.
