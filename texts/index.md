## Conflicting dates and records

- Q-Con (11 September) came before the formal NI report launch (23 September): the findings were presented first, the report published later.
- The Q-Con programme spells the firm 'Quantum Forsight'; the correct spelling is Quantum Foresight.
- Neither the EU record for SAFIRE nor SAMI's own post uses the words 'peer-reviewed'. SAMI says the scenarios were reviewed within the Directorate-General and are third party endorsed; this archive uses that wording.
- The EU record names individuals, not firms. The SAMI affiliation of the named authors comes from SAMI's own materials.
- The Futures Toolkit has a 2017 edition and a 2023 refresh; SAMI's role is documented for the 2023 refresh.
- The misuse assessment and the delivery backend have no date in the public record.
