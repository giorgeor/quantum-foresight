## SAFIRE scenarios (2021) (SAMI prior work)

Foresight method; built; published; work id safire

**What:** SAMI's contribution to SAFIRE, the European Commission's Strategic Foresight Intelligence System for EU research and innovation, published by DG Research and Innovation on 9 August 2021: 44 scenarios, four global plus four for each of ten global regions. Jonathan Blanchard Smith and Huw Williams are among the named authors.

**Threads:** foresight method

## NI Quantum Foresight project (2026)

Government advisory; built; completed; work id ni-project

**What:** Mapped Northern Ireland's quantum capabilities and supply chains, compared other regions, ran a SWOT and an economic impact model, and set out tailored recommendations. Report launched 23 September 2026.

**Summary:** Produced by SAMI Consulting for Matrix NI, commissioned for the Department for the Economy. Findings were presented at Q-Con Gdansk on 11 September; the formal launch followed at AICON Belfast, by the NI Minister for the Economy.

**Threads:** translation gap; scenario reports

**Led to** Q-Con Gdansk sessions (2026): the findings were presented at Q-Con on 11 September, before the formal report launch on 23 September. Evidence (they say so): Q-Con programme lists 'Northern Ireland Quantum Report and Recommendations' (source 3); launch date from source 7
